  http://127.0.0.1:18090/

Browsers communicate only with Portal. Portal proxies Flink UIs internally using
Service DNS. The ServiceAccount reads Kubernetes resources; services/proxy is
not granted. Target namespaces must already exist and be reachable from Portal.
Portal has no login. Configure authentication at your Ingress before exposing it
outside a trusted test network.
